# Paladin Judgements corrections

- Judgement of Light heals for 2% of the attacker's base health, independent of stamina or maximum-health buffs.
- Light's healing triggers from melee auto attacks and ranged auto shots; spell attacks and periodic damage do not trigger it.
- Light, Wisdom and Justice enforce one active Judgement per Paladin across targets. Another Paladin's Judgement is preserved.
- Learning a first Judgement automatically includes Judgement Mastery for 2 Ability Essence total. Additional Judgements cost 1 Ability Essence each and no additional Ability Gems.
- Existing Ghostly Blow, Ghostly Strike, Riposte and other deployed fixes are retained.

This is a server-only repair. No client download is required. Existing VERIFIED/CERTIFIED labels and tester certification records are unchanged. Offline engineering checks cover the repaired paths; gameplay certification remains with testers.
