# Demoralizing Banner, party bots, and Featured Build rewards

- Demoralizing Banner displays a warrior banner that its owner can select for Intervene.
- Party bots catch up to their human leader between fights and learn the abilities and talent ranks granted by their accepted Featured Build.
- Characters who received the rewards for their active Featured Build keep their equipped Mystic Enchants when deactivating it. Leaving an unfinished build still clears temporary enchants.
