# Cryotherapist healer bot

- Healer requests can now recruit Cryotherapist. It follows its GM Featured Build and uses the Theradrim Shardling, Mystical Elements heirlooms, a staff and a healing libram.
- Cryotherapist shields and heals the tank, dispels harmful effects, builds Spirit Frost with frost attacks, and can resurrect party members. It equips Pendant of the Sorcerer and two Rings of the Seer.
