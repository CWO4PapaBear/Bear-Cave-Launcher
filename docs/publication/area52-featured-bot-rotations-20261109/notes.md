# Featured dungeon bot builds and rotations

- Requesting damage can now recruit a White Walker with a Theradrim Shardling or a ranged Marksman. Tank requests can recruit Bulwark.
- A request prefers an available bot already in the requested role. An idle bot can prepare another supported role when needed.
- The six registered archetypes learn their abilities, talents, Primary Stat and Mystic Enchants from the linked GM Featured Build for the requested level. Bots already in a party keep their accepted revision.
- GMs receive a diagnostic when a linked revision fails validation. A bot can reuse its last accepted revision if it still passes at the requested level.
