# Area 52 — Deathbringer corrections

- Deathbringer can now trigger from single-target periodic damage as well as direct damage, with 100% chance and its existing shared three-second internal cooldown.
- Unbounded Deathbringer triggers from direct single-target or area damage, never periodic damage. Its proc hits the triggering enemy and valid enemies within eight yards.
- Unbounded enforces a separate four-second cooldown for each affected enemy and each caster. Overlapping hits cannot repeatedly trigger the same target during that cooldown.
- Both retain their existing independent damage and healing formulas and Drain Life modifier behavior. Neither now converts damage dealt into a percentage heal.
- Unbounded's enchant connection and engineering VERIFIED label are corrected. Existing Certified records are preserved; engineering Verification does not claim tester Certification.
- Updated tooltips explain the trigger rules, radius and cooldowns.

Engineering checks were performed offline. Live gameplay Certification remains with the testers.
