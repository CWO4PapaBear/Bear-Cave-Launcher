# Featured Draft rewards and Primary Stat weapon bonuses

Featured Draft Vanity rewards now correctly recognize milestones already delivered after a character reload. Affected eligible characters automatically catch up on assigned rewards they have reached, without resending previously claimed milestones. Existing reward receipts and collection ownership are preserved.

All eight Primary Stat weapon bonuses have completed engineering Verification: Devastating Strikes, Heavy Swings, Agile Strikes, Fatal Wounds, Devastating Spells, Magic Acceleration, Empowered Mending and Spiritual Acceleration.

Right-click your Primary Stat in Character Advancement to certify each weapon bonus separately after testing. Check its effects with the required weapon, weapon changes, logout/login, and removal when switching paths. Certifying a Primary Stat does not automatically certify its bonuses. Existing Certified records remain unchanged.
