# The Bear Cave - Updates

## Cryotherapist

- Frostbolt and Ice Lance now trigger Cryotherapy on up to five nearby allies, prioritizing the lowest health. It heals for 2% of damage immediately and each second for two seconds, with the target-health cap preserved.
- Frozen Light, Lesser Frozen Light and all eight Icy Penance ranks are connected. Icy Spirit's splash is centered on your Frozen Light recipient.
- Spirit Frost supports Flash Heal, Greater Heal and Prayer of Healing. Each consumed stack reduces cast time by 53% and mana cost by 10%; two stacks make the cast instant and 20% cheaper. Failed casts do not spend stacks.
- Direct Holy healing grants Fingers of Light. Its bonus is consumed when Fingers of Frost successfully procs.
- Cryotherapist has completed engineering Verification and is available for tester certification after this deployment. Existing Certified records are preserved.

Update through the Bear Cave Launcher after publication to receive the matching tooltip changes.
