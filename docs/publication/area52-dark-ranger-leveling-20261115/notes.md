Dark Ranger Leveling

Dark Ranger's leveling abilities, talents, and Mystic Enchants have received engineering verification. Chimera Shot now handles the hunter's Stings correctly, Soul Harvest extends with afflicted enemies, and Frenzy Shot's pet cleave no longer rolls a second critical hit.

Dark Arrow has a 20% proc chance and summons an aggressive skeleton for 12 seconds. Shadowfiend and the temporary Raise Dead ghoul can coexist with your primary pet, totems, and skeletons. Eye of Kilrogg restores your controls and pet when possession ends.

Characters who know Auto Shot can start it by damaging their selected hostile target in combat. Manual cancellation lasts until the next direct attack. Bone Arrow can start Auto Shot independently of Dark Ranger.

Roothound Shadow, Briar, and Solar Bite remember your autocast choice through rank changes, resummoning, and login.

Verified abilities: Army of the Dead, Chimera Shot, Eye of Kilrogg, Frenzy Shot, Kill Command, Raise Dead, Seed of Corruption, Silence, Soul Harvest, and Void Eruption.

Verified talents: Everlasting Affliction, Hunting Party, Shadow Reach, and Thrill of the Hunt.

Eligible tester collections gain the Verified Mystic Enchants Careful Aim, Darkness, Hunter vs. Wild, and Long Shadows. Previously collected enchants are preserved.
