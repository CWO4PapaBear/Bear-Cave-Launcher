# Area 52 Ghostly Blow and Ghostly Strike corrections

Ghostly Blow now replaces Riposte when its Mystic Enchant is equipped and the required ability is owned. Removing the enchant restores Riposte and preserves independently owned spells and other grants.

The strike retains its existing 125% weapon damage as Shadow, 40-energy cost, six-second cooldown and no parry prerequisite. Its periodic Shadow damage uses the existing level scaling and 11.1% spell-power coefficient.

Every three seconds, periodic damage and refresh require the original living caster to remain within eight yards with the enchant and Riposte still owned. Moving out of range suppresses damage and refresh; the five-second debuff expires naturally. Losing the caster or required ownership ends the effect at its next periodic check. The recursive refresh-helper route is suppressed. Improved Riposte reduces Ghostly Strike's remaining cooldown by one second only when Ghostly Blow actually deals periodic damage; fully absorbed or zero damage does not qualify.

This package also includes the already qualified, previously pending Ghostly Strike correction: 125% normal, 180% dagger and 100% two-handed weapon damage; an eight-second 10-point dodge buff or parry with a single two-handed weapon and empty offhand; and Improved Riposte's 33%/66%/100% chance after Riposte to reduce Ghostly Strike cooldown by three seconds. Prior Riposte and Blademaster repairs remain included.

No client or DLL update is needed. Existing VERIFIED/CERTIFIED labels and certification records remain unchanged; these repairs alone do not increase progress totals. Engineering qualification is offline; live gameplay certification remains with testers.

Source publication is a scoped patch on a feature branch, not a merge to main. These notes are published only after owner activation and readiness.
