# Area 52 - Crusader Strike and training dummies

- Crusader Strike deals Physical damage only across all eight active ranks. Tooltips now say Physical explicitly. Its corrected weapon-damage formula, three charges and four-second recharge remain unchanged.
- Standard and advanced training dummies are permanently immune to crowd control. The configurable dummy can no longer disable that protection.
- Boss Simulator retains its stun/disarm heavy-strike counter window. When the strike lands or is stopped, it enters a ten-second CC-immune phase with an alert. When immunity ends, an alert announces Shadow Bolt and the dummy immediately starts casting it. The next heavy-strike warning follows six seconds after the bolt starts.
- Existing verification and Certified labels are unchanged. Earlier ability-batch fixes remain in place.

Update through the Bear Cave Launcher after publication for the corrected Crusader Strike tooltips. No DLL changes are included.
