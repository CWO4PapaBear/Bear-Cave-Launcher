# Cryotherapist Theradrim Shardling

Cryotherapist displays a Theradrim Shardling alongside the character in character creation. While following the Cryotherapist Featured build, learning Tether Elemental unlocks Raise Elemental. Its first cast grants a Theradrim Shardling when the player has no elemental companion. An existing elemental takes priority.

The Guardian of Time opens all three heirloom shops for any character whose account has a character at the realm level cap. Discerning Eye of the Beast and Swift Hand of Justice equip silently. Their on-kill mana and healing effects still apply.
