# Area 52 Demonic Leap wings, blessings and replacement interactions

- Demonic Leap now activates its existing demonic wings visual on each successful leap, including reactivation. Movement and cooldown behavior are unchanged.
- Different Paladin blessings can now coexist when cast by the same player on Area 52. Stronger Might/Wisdom still replace weaker copies; duplicate-rank and same-blessing restrictions remain. Other realm modes retain their original blessing rule.
- Hoplite's Thrust now qualifies for Tides of Battle through its active Sinister Strike replacement link, including all twelve ranks. Pre-cast energy determines Ebb or Flow, and a cast cannot apply the proc twice.
- Shared spell modifiers and proc-family eligibility now recognize active original abilities, including chained replacements. Existing resource, school, hit, ownership and cooldown conditions remain.
- Fighter's Sinister Strike and Slice and Ice's critical-hit interaction use the same eligibility lookup.
- Wind Slam now recognizes authorized replacements for its triggering spell family and eligible consuming abilities. Its shield-specific slow follows the same replacement chain.
- Draconic Sinister now recognizes chained replacements while retaining its existing proc chance and cooldown restrictions.
- Replacement abilities keep their own damage, costs and effects. No verification labels or tester certification records change. Other custom scripts still require individual interaction-path review; this is not a claim that every replacement interaction is complete.

Engineering validation uses offline native checks; live gameplay certification remains with testers. This frozen patch is relative to the qualified deployed source, not an unreviewed merge of the general checkout.
