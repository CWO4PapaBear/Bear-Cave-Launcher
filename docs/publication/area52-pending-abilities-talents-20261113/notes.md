# Area 52 Ability and Talent Repairs

Shadowfiend now restores 30% of the summoner’s base mana per proc. Metamorphosis teaches Shadow Cleave, Challenging Howl, Demon Charge and Immolation Aura for use in demon form. Its damage bonus is 15% against creatures and 10% against players.

Blood Horror responds to direct enemy damage within 15 yards and protects the channel against interrupts, silences and stuns. Improved Shadowform, Careful Aim, Shadow Power, Mental Quickness, Siphon Life and Soul Siphon now apply their reviewed rank and ownership rules.
