# Area 52 — Masteries, Alacrity, Bountiful Gift and Roothounds

Learning a member of a Mastery group also selects its Mastery under the normal budget rules. Alacrity grants 2/4/6% spell, melee and ranged haste. Bountiful Gift gives Mark of the Wild a 15-second cooldown while equipped and retains its quadrupled attribute bonus and 30-second duration.

Two saddle-free Wardrobe pets join the Dark Rootbound Beast. Rootbound Beast uses Briar Bite with Nature and Bleed effects. Sunbleached Roothound uses Solar Bite with Fire and Bleed effects. Dark Rootbound Beast uses Shadow Bite with Shadow and Bleed effects. The pale mount keeps its identity and is renamed Sunbleached Roothound.
