# Defias Gunslinger - Verified

Defias Gunslinger is now Verified and available to eligible tester collections. Pistol Shot grants Gunslinger’s Strike and readies Pirate’s Flurry. Grape Shot hits up to five enemies, with additional weapon damage on the main target. Character Creation now lists Gunslinger’s Strike immediately after Pistol Shot at level 1 and Grape Shot at level 18.
