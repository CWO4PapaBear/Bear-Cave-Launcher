# The Bear Cave - Updates

## Dungeon companions and test duels

- Recruit an available same-faction bot with `.playerbots coa tank`, `.playerbots coa heal` or `.playerbots coa dps`. The existing `lfg bot` recruitment channel also works.
- The bot prepares at your level (15–80), keeps its assigned archetype, and whispers when ready for your normal party invitation and summon. Dungeon commitments, existing owners and test-bench holds prevent double booking.
- Recruited party bots support the existing owner-controlled duel behavior. Whisper `leave` to release a bot; ending a duel does not dismiss it automatically.
- The existing20-bot pool stays at10 per faction. Wrath Dungeon Finder and the earlier Arena1v1 work are preserved.

## Permanent archetype equipment and appearance

- Hoplite uses Hopstrike's equipment; Mystic Knight uses Mktest's equipment.
- Naturalist uses Preened Ironfeather armor and cloak, two Rings of the Seer, Pendant of the Sorcerer, Paladin Libram of Healing, Dignified Headmaster's Charge, Swift Hand of Justice and Stick on a Carrot.
- Assigned item instances are retained when bots change level. Heirlooms scale through the native item system; equipment is not randomly regenerated for each request.
- Bots use their archetype's primary Vanity armor set through the existing appearance system, without replacing functional equipment.

No ability, talent or Mystic Enchant Verification/Certification labels change. No client update or addon is required. This document is staged for publication only after successful activation and server readiness.
