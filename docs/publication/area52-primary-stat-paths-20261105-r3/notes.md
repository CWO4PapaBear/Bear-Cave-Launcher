# Primary Stat Paths

Strength grants parry rating equal to 50% of Strength on the Strength path. Ordinary parry chance caps at 75%. Positive direct-parry buffs lasting up to 20 seconds can take it above that ceiling.

Agility grants melee Attack Power at the ranged rate. Agility Primary doubles Agility's contribution to melee and ranged critical strike chance. When an eligible attack critically hits, only the damage supplied by Agility-derived Attack Power receives the extra bonus.

Intellect Primary grants 2 damage power and 1 healing power per point of generic Spell Power. Spirit Primary grants 1 damage power and 2 healing power instead. Damage-only and healing-only bonuses retain their ordinary values.

New Rockbiter imbues last two hours. A two-handed weapon receives double the base attack power. Intellect and Spirit paths receive 33% of the resulting amount, rounded down per weapon. Recast an older imbue to receive the corrected scaling.

## Equipment bonuses

One-handed and two-handed path bonuses now follow the equipped weapon requirements. Agile Strikes reduces eligible damaging melee and ranged ability costs and global cooldowns by 8%. Heavy Swings and Fatal Wounds apply to their eligible abilities.

## Path-dependent abilities

Spirit Primary supplies the advertised dispel cooldowns. Cleanse Spirit and Abolish Poison, Curse and Disease have no cooldown, Cleanse and Dispel Magic have a three-second cooldown, and Purify has a five-second cooldown. Other applicable cooldown modifiers still apply. The non-Spirit arena restrictions are enforced.

Cat Form clears movement restrictions on Strength and Agility. Tree of Life clears them on Spirit. Spirit's Blessed Resilience requires an incoming critical hit, applies its 20-second internal cooldown, and limits its healing bonus to Holy healing. Titan's Grip applies its Spirit healing penalty as the path, talent and equipment change.
