# Area 52 cross-faction cooperation

- Alliance and Horde players can form parties and raids, use dungeon-finder queues together, and communicate through cross-faction chat and channels.
- Cross-faction trade, mail, friend requests and friend online visibility are enabled. The player list can show both factions.
- Alliance, Horde and neutral Auction Houses share one listing pool. Existing items, bids, buyouts, owners, paid deposits and expiration times are retained. The realm's existing faction fees remain: 5% sales cut and the same faction deposit rates.
- Dungeon Wisdomball offers no longer reject players solely for faction. Scoped objective and dungeon-area checks are corrected. Ashcrombe receives the missing attack protections in Shadowfang Keep.
- Cross-faction battleground queues are enabled, retaining racial appearance and restoring normal faction on leaving. The imported battleground group-queue limit is three players; ordinary party size is unchanged. Rated arenas and Wintergrasp are unchanged.

Guilds, calendar access, auctions' underlying fee data, arena teams and open-world quest eligibility are otherwise unchanged. Existing Area 52 combat fixes are retained. Bots are not included in this package and will be reconciled separately.

No client download is required. No ability, talent or Mystic Enchant verification/certification labels change.

Engineering validation: native build/unit, 15 source suites, focused native behavior checks, production-schema SQL apply/rollback/retry checks, configuration rollback, exact source patch reconstruction and package validation. Live gameplay acceptance remains with testers.
