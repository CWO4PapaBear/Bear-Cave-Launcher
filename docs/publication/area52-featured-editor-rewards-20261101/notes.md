# Featured Draft Editor and Vanity Rewards

- GMs can configure Featured Draft armor and weapons with a character preview and the existing Wardrobe, including a full armor set. Full-set selection preserves separately assigned weapons and mounts. Vanity relics are excluded.
- Ground and flying mounts can be assigned from the Vanity tab. Unassigned rewards remain empty; no replacement mount is chosen automatically.
- A separate AI Bot Rotation field follows Rotation. It stores instructions with the build for future bot integration; this release does not execute that text or change bot combat logic.
- Eligible Featured Draft characters receive assigned Vanity rewards by mail: shoulders at 10, chest at 13, gloves at 16, ground mount at 20, weapons at 22, head at 25, boots at 28, bracers at 36, belt at 40, back at 45, legs at 50, and flying mount at 60. Each milestone is issued once per character. Leaving or customizing the Draft stops future rewards; previously mailed rewards remain.
- Before level 10, selecting a Featured Draft in the Build Architect applies the same Draft rules as character creation. Only the final qualifying Draft grants its Mystic Enchants at maximum level. Advancement changes now warn before abandoning a Draft.
- Cryotherapist uses Frostfire Remembrance (Blue). Cryomancer returns to Frostbitten Staff and retains its existing mount.

No ability, talent or Mystic Enchant verification/certification totals change in this release.
