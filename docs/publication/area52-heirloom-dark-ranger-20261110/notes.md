# Dark Ranger Legendary Mystic Enchant

Dark Ranger is Verified and available in eligible tester collections. Shadow Bolt refreshes Dark Arrows to six charges. Eligible shots build Shadow Shells for Shadow Artillery. Darkfall increases damage by 20% per stack, and casting at three stacks consumes the buff. Artillery shares impact damage among enemies in its 8-yard circle. Each enemy receives the full periodic damage.

Owned heirlooms can be delivered to your bags from Vanity. At the Guardian of Time, each missing heirloom costs 400 Runes in a set cache. Completing the set grants the cache for free. Accounts that already completed a set can receive its cache on login.
