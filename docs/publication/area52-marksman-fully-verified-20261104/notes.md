# Marksman Leveling - Fully Verified

LiterallyElbowTTV's Marksman Leveling build is ready for tester certification across its abilities, talents, and Mystic Enchants.

- Corrected several Hunter shot damage formulas, proc conditions, and talent interactions.
- Improved Slice and Dice grants ranged attack speed only with Compound Power, using the final melee-speed bonus.
- Rapid Fire's creature damage bonus applies to Hunter shots. Its tooltip now states that restriction.
- Feign Death retains its special threat-reduction behavior against raid-instance bosses.
- Aspect of the Viper and Mana-Forged Barrier now replace each other when cast.
- Corrected poison durations and scaling, including Instant Poison's 6% attack-power contribution.
- Improved pet ability ownership and restoration, stance cleanup, and stealth-related effects.
- Updated tooltips and Verified labels while preserving existing tester certifications.

Verified Mystic Enchants use the existing tester collection grants. Accounts deliberately excluded from those grants remain excluded.
