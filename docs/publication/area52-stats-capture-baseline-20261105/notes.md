# Capture Baseline in the Stats panel

Open your character's Stats panel and click **Capture Baseline** before changing an ability, talent, or Mystic Enchant. The list shows what changed, including hit bonuses, school-specific spell stats, learned abilities, and visible aura stacks. Hover a row for the before-and-after values. Capture again to start a fresh comparison, or click **Clear** to return to the full sheet.
