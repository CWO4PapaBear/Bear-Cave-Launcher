# Area 52 — Cache Reward Updates

- Prestige and Callboard caches use the realm's maximum level to select a level-60, 70, or 80 equipment catalog. Eligible dungeon, raid, world, quest and vendor gear is included; crafted items are excluded.
- Equipment rewards stay within your equipped average item level through six item levels above it. Listed item levels are used; heirlooms follow upstream's zero contribution. A cache stays unopened when no eligible reward exists in that range.
- Rewards prioritize weapons, armor, then accessories, followed by your build role, selected Primary Stat, matching learned abilities in set bonuses, and item stats. Equally ranked eligible items have equal chances; raid tiers do not receive separate weighting.
- Disabling a tier's main raid excludes the whole tier, including vendor and alternate-source rewards. Individual raid difficulties also govern availability.
- Items already held in inventory, banks, mail or auctions are excluded. If you own an appearance but no longer have the item, choose whether to receive it again or request another item.
- Prestige retains its 25% tier-token roll and the existing 5% conditional chance for a second token. Tokens must have a supported redemption route and match eligible gear. Emblems and deprecated tokens are not substituted for tier tokens. Callboard's separate gold schedule is preserved.
- Relevant raid-set gear and token redemption no longer require a classic class. Other equipment and quest requirements remain in force. Item data refreshes automatically after login.
- The entire reward bundle must fit in your bags. A private Raid Warning explains insufficient bag space; the cache and saved reward remain unchanged for retry. Rewards are not mailed.
- A Prestige character who already owns every eligible item for the current build receives 10,000 Bazaar Tokens instead. Declining rewards or having no reward in range does not qualify for that payout.

This update does not change Manastorm's independent reward schedule or tester certification records.
