# Total Eclipse and Relentless Strikes

Gaining Solar or Lunar Eclipse with Total Eclipse triggers Owlkin Frenzy. The next Wrath or Starsurge during Solar applies Insect Swarm. The next Starfire or Starsurge during Lunar applies Moonfire.

Relentless Strikes respects its named targets. With Improved Sinister Strike at 2/2, Thrust and Pistol Shot retain their 40 Energy cost.
