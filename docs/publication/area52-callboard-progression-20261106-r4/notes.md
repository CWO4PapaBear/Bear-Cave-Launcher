# Callboard Progression and Daily Bounties

From level 60, both faction boards offer outdoor hunts and Wanted quests in one zone each day. The board switches to Outland at 70 and Northrend at 80. Previously accepted quests can still be turned in.

Open a Callboard cache to receive eligible dungeon or raid gear for your current tier and equipped average item level, up to six item levels higher. Dungeon and raid release restrictions apply. Temporal Contracts continue to support eligible repeat completions through Complete Now.
