# The Bear Cave - Updates

Improved Sprint's 3/3 capstone now removes slows when Sprint activates. While Sprint is active, incoming root durations are reduced by 50% and slows cannot reduce movement below Area 52's 115% base movement speed. These protections end with Sprint or when the capstone is removed. Rank 1 and 2 retain their existing duration bonuses without receiving the capstone. Existing roots are not removed by the slow cleanse.

Talent and buff tooltips now say "base movement speed." Existing verification and certification labels are preserved.

Includes the pending Mastery UI correction: member abilities no longer display rarity-gem costs; the Mastery retains its own gem cost and members retain Ability Essence costs. Extra Fire Totem explanatory paragraphs are removed. Actual purchase costs and rarity colors are unchanged.

Previously deployed fixes, including mounted-speed corrections, Invocation and Raid Warning, are preserved.

Area 52 public in-game bug reports now go to our server repository. Use Help → Bug Report and choose public reporting; reports have a two-minute per-account cooldown. Do not include passwords or personal information in public reports.
