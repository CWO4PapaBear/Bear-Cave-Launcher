- **Spellblade:** Replenishment now works when the recipient is completely out of mana. Retains the 4/8/12% melee proc, 7/14/20% base-mana return and rank-three swing-preservation capstone.
- **Power Surge:** Engineering checks cover its 2/4/6% proc chance, spell-school bursts and Invocation damage/absorption return over ten seconds.
- **Spiritual Attunement:** Mana-Forged Barrier doubles the mana gained from effective allied healing. Self-healing and overhealing do not grant mana.
- **Soul Link:** Applies to eligible summoned and enslaved demons, excludes summoned Imps, and follows ownership and its 30-yard range. Redirected damage cannot be prevented on the demon.
- **Master Demonologist:** Corrects enslaved-demon bonuses and their cleanup. Restores the first-rank Voidwalker bonus's 100-yard range to match the other ranks.
- **Heart of the Crusader:** Restores the rank-three Judgement burst, capped at five enemies within eight yards of the target. Its damage inherits applicable Judgement damage modifiers; its AP/SP contribution is halved against players.
- **Improved Consecration:** Restores the instant Holy hit and attack-speed slow for normal Consecration and Emanate. The instant hit generates 85% extra threat. Its slow does not stack with equivalent attack-speed slows; its damage-reduction benefit remains intact.
- **Ardent Defender:** Implements the rank-specific rescue chance, capped at 15% maximum-health absorption. It only rescues when that absorption prevents death. Successful rescues retain the heal, three-second healing bonus and shared two-minute cooldown with Cheat Death and Spirit of Redemption, including across relogging.
- **Ritual of Summoning:** High-Risk characters cannot cast, assist with or receive ritual summons. Eligibility is rechecked before completion and when accepting a request; the shared meeting-stone ritual helpers use the same protection.
