# Area 52 Patch Notes - Realm Bank and Bank Tabs

- Realm Bank is available in the Vanity menu for 3,500 Bazaar Tokens.
- Tiraxis sells Personal Bank Tab Vouchers for 7,500 tokens and Realm Bank Tab Vouchers for 9,600 tokens.
- Personal and Realm Banks support up to six tabs each.
- Fixed partial-stack deposits and withdrawals.
- Soulbound items are blocked from Realm Bank deposits to prevent them becoming stuck. Personal Bank storage is unchanged.
