# Area 52 - Art of War, Angelic Feather and Brutal Pummel corrections

- Art of War now has a 50% chance to trigger from normal or critical damage dealt by Judgement, Crusader Strike, Execution Sentence, Divine Storm and main-hand auto-attacks, matching its tooltip at all three active ranks.
- Eligible ability replacements retain their inherited interaction. Execution Sentence damage is included. Off-hand auto-attacks, unrelated damage, healing and attacks that deal no damage cannot trigger it.
- Successful procs have a six-second internal cooldown. The existing rank-specific 33%/66%/100% cast-time reduction buffs remain unchanged.
- Angelic Feather once again shows its ground visual. The first eligible player still consumes the feather; restoring the visual does not restore the old repeating pickup effect.
- Brutal Pummel now triggers only from Pummel and eligible replacements. Rank 1 has a 50% chance to apply two Sunder Armor stacks; rank 2 guarantees them. Pacify requires the caster to be below 35% health. The rank-2 capstone deals 35% attack-power base damage on a successful interrupt. Unrelated melee attacks no longer trigger its effects.
- Previously deployed Physical Crusader Strike and training dummy corrections are preserved. No client download, SQL changes or verification/Certified label changes are included.
