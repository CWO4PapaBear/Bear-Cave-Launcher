# Area 52 Ability and Mystic Enchant Repairs

- Blood Arrow and Shadow Arrow now start Auto Shot like Steady Shot. Switching targets or leaving combat stops Auto Shot.
- Frenzy Shot lets your pet cleave up to five additional enemies. Void Eruption extends its buff for each enemy hit and applies your learned Shadow Word: Pain in an eligible form.
- Long Shadows reduces Physical and Shadow ability costs by 4%. Overlapping reductions from these enchants use the strongest applicable bonus.
- Seed of Corruption detonates at its damage threshold or on death and can trigger nearby Seeds.
- Silence cannot miss. Each Vampiric Embrace heal is capped at 5% of the caster’s maximum health.
