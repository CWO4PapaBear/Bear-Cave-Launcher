# Area 52 Patch Notes - Worldforged Mystic Enchants

- All 990 rare enchants in the Fighter's, Wizard's, Primal, Demonic, Draconic, Gigantic, Necrotic and Element-Bound families are prepared for VERIFIED tester access. Existing CERTIFIED records are preserved.
- Proc chances now follow each enchant's spell-specific record. Damage-over-time effects roll per damaging tick per target; direct damage rolls per target hit.
- Additional copies increase the bonuses by 100% of their base value per copy, without adding proc rolls. Eligible enchants compete in a randomized order, and the first successful proc starts their shared 20-second cooldown.
- Necrotic enchants require the named ability to deliver the killing blow. Gigantic and Necrotic damage bonuses retain their above-75% and below-35% target-health conditions.
- Primal Power follows your chosen Strength, Agility, Intellect or Spirit path. Element-Bound reduces magic-school spell costs.
- Active power buffs and the shared cooldown retain their remaining time through logout; time spent offline counts toward expiration.
- Tooltips show the actual chances, bonuses and durations. Verified enchants become available through the existing temporary tester collection access after login.

Update through the launcher and log in again to test. VERIFIED means engineering checks passed; CERTIFIED remains tester-owned.
