# 🎁 Area 52 Patch Notes — Cache Rewards Upgraded!

**More useful loot. Better progression. Rewards all the way to level 80!**

### ⚔️ Callboard caches: rewards that fit your character
- Expanded the eligible dungeon and raid reward catalogs, including additional confirmed item variants.
- Improved upgrade selection and removed the fallback that could hand out lower-level gear when no eligible upgrade was available. If there is no eligible reward, your cache stays with you.
- Weapon rewards now account for **shields, dual wielding, and two-handed weapons**, with preference for your lowest eligible equipped slots.
- Added expansion-specific reward categories while respecting the realm’s available content. Outland and Northrend Mythic rewards remain **Coming Soon**.
- Fixed a crash while preparing cache equipment rewards and improved delivery of pending rewards.

### 💚💙 Adventurer’s Caches get a boost
- **Green caches** can now award suitable uncommon armor, weapons, and accessories through level 80.
- **Blue caches** can now award suitable rare equipment through level 80, retaining the existing eligible green fallback.
- Every green cache includes **1–10 Runes of Descension AND 1–10 Bazaar Tokens**.
- Every blue cache includes **5–15 Runes of Descension AND 5–15 Bazaar Tokens**.
- Existing food, drinks, potions, and materials remain in the reward pools.

### 🗺️ More rewards for questing
**2,567 eligible level 61–80 quests** now include an Adventurer’s reward using the existing lower-level pattern: white satchels, green caches, or blue caches. Existing quest rewards are preserved.

### 🚀 Before you jump in
**Close the game and reopen the launcher to receive the client update.** Then head back to Area 52 and try the refreshed rewards!

If a cache behaves unexpectedly, please report the **character, cache name, and item received**. Thank you for helping improve Area 52! 🐻
