# Holy spell talent corrections and Exorcist's Slash

- Divine Favor applies to eligible Holy spells across classes. Its critical-chance effects no longer cancel each other, and its 50% healing bonus does not increase damage.
- Divine Illumination's Holy healing critical bonus includes other classes' Holy healing spells. Its resource-cost reduction is preserved.
- Spiritual Focus's capstone responds to an enemy interrupt while you are idle, grants its stated protection and next-Holy-spell critical bonus, and observes its internal cooldown.
- Light's Grace recognizes both Holy Light and Prayer of Healing, including eligible replacements.
- Area 52's active Exorcist's Slash deals Holy damage, accepts an active Art of War proc of any rank, and remains instant. Its tooltip states these requirements.
- Exorcist's Slash retains Exorcism modifiers, grants the appropriate learned rank, and dispatches its random cooldown or charge benefit. Its cooldown against players doubles the actual modified cooldown.
- Sanctity of Battle can trigger from Exorcist's Slash. Its empowerment is retained for delayed effects and consumed by the eligible ability.
- Righteous Vengeance recognizes Exorcist's Slash through Exorcism targeting and applies its documented Strength-path multiplier.
- Both Exorcist's Slash families are eligible for Divine Favor. The inactive family remains unavailable on Area 52.

Exorcist's Slash has completed engineering Verification. Existing Certified labels are preserved. No inactive Mystic Enchant is enabled by this update.


## Stormwind Runeforge

The newly placed Stormwind Runeforge gains the same map/minimap marker as Orgrimmar. Ask a Stormwind City, Harbor or Stormwind Guard for The Runeforge to mark its location. Existing Orgrimmar directions and both objects remain unchanged.
