# Featured Mount Selection and Riding Requirements

- Featured Build editors can select every Vanity mount labeled as flying, including Archmage's Prismatic Disc (Arcane) and Farseer's Raging Tempest (Arcane).
- Mount items no longer require a character level. Existing riding skills, required spells, and flight-zone restrictions still apply.
- Featured mount rewards are still mailed at levels 20 and 60.
- No ability, talent, enchant Verification or Certification totals change.
- Mystic Knight keeps Mystic Runesaber as its ground reward and uses Archmage's Prismatic Disc (Arcane) as its flying reward.
