# Worldforged Pickup Placements

- Corrected 648 existing Worldforged pickups, including items buried under terrain or floating above their intended spots.
- Restored 93 missing pickups and 24 supporting objects. Hid 576 obsolete spawns while retaining their database records.

## Dark Rootbound Beast

The Beastmaster's Whistle adds a saddle-free Dark Rootbound Beast to the Wardrobe's Boar category. The pet shares the Felhound's family and has its own Shadow Bite.
