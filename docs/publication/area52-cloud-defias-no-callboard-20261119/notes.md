# ☁️⚔️ AREA 52 — A NEW HOME IN THE CLOUD!
## A bigger world. A stronger foundation. A new chapter of testing.

🚀 **WELCOME TO THE CLOUD TEST REALM**
Area 52 now has a dedicated cloud home, separate from our local Development Server. Your accounts and characters came with us, preserving existing Cloud progress for the GM accounts already testing there. Future repairs will be developed locally and delivered to you in reviewed releases.

📥 **CLOSE AND REOPEN YOUR LAUNCHER**
Let the Bear Cave Launcher update, select **Area 52 – Free Pick Alpha Test**, then press **Play** to receive the release's client files and Cloud connection settings. Keep normal tester client updates enabled. No manual IP editing is needed when using the updated launcher.

🌍 **UPSTREAM MERGED. AREA 52 FREE-PICK PRESERVED.**
We've brought the reviewed upstream COA Core into our Area 52 fork while preserving our Free-Pick gameplay and custom work. The merged foundation includes **Pale Reach**, **Manastorms**, upstream **COA class, spell and talent fixes**, and world, quest, creature and dungeon improvements.

These are new opportunities to explore and test—not a claim that every upstream feature has completed Area 52 certification. Tell us where something doesn't behave as expected!

🔫 **FEATURED BUILD — DEFIAS GUNSLINGER — FULLY VERIFIED**
This release completes the build's engineering verification, including **Embracing Shadows, Blinding Dispersion, Lone Wolf, Veiled Shadows, Sidearm Specialization, Savage Combat, Lifebloom, Savage Strikes and Vigor**.

Expect repaired talent and enchant interactions, restored stat bonuses, and corrected verification labels. **Verified means engineering checks passed. Certified remains tester-owned through in-game testing.**

🤖 **A GENTLER BOT ARRIVAL**
After the first player enters, bots wait one minute, then log in in balanced groups of five at one-minute intervals: **1 Tank, 1 Healer and 3 DPS**. This spreads their arrival out instead of bringing the entire population online together. The roster remains at **20 bots**.

📝 **CLEARER TESTING TOOLS**
New bug reports include the submitting character's name. The in-game certification scoreboard has been removed; certification functions and existing records remain. Tooltip corrections are included for **Defias Gunslinger** and **Careful Aim**.

🛠️ **A BETTER FOUNDATION FOR PERSONAL PRIVATE SERVERS**
Our existing GitHub fork brings the merged core and Area 52 work together, with reproducible source and migration history. That makes it a clearer starting point for developers who want to build their own personal private server. Setup and compatible client data are still required.
https://github.com/CWO4PapaBear/azerothcore-wotlk-coa
