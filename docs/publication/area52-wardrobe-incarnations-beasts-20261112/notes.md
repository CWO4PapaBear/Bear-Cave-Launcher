# Area 52 Wardrobe

- Felguards can display their selected Wardrobe weapon. Eligible forms and summoned pets use their assigned incarnation appearances.
- Choosing an eligible Tamed Beast in the Wardrobe recalls the matching stabled pet or creates the selected beast when there is room to park your current pet.
- Temporary Featured Draft combat pets keep their Draft reward rules. Wardrobe selection cannot replace an active loan.
