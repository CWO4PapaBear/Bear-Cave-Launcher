# Area 52 Patch Notes - Callboard Temporal Contracts

- The Callboard now offers Temporal Contracts. Each contract type earns one completion per day during the week. Unused completions carry forward until the weekly reset. A matching quest already in your log reserves a completion.
- Use **Complete Now** to spend either gold or Bazaar Tokens. The gold price is twice the quest's gold reward, with a 10-gold minimum. The token price is twice its Bazaar Token reward, with a 50-token minimum.
- Heroic and Mythic dungeon contracts require an available Classic dungeon at that difficulty. The Callboard continues to respect quest eligibility and the realm's existing raid release schedule.
- Forty-four Callboard dailies now award 30, 40, or 50 Bazaar Tokens. Heroic and Mythic Dungeon Diving still award their existing caches.
- Plague's Predatory Pests appears in the PvE quest group. Two Warchief's Command trackers now point to the correct destinations: Zoram'gar Outpost and Ratchet.
