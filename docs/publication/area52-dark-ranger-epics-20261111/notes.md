# Dark Ranger Epic Enchants and Auto Shot

- Shadow Waste Bolt follows the Shadow Bolt ranks you know, including after signing back in.
- Dark Arrow can summon a Mindless Skeleton when a marked enemy dies.
- Physical ranged hits have a 33% chance to grant a Bone Arrow charge, up to three. Bone Arrow spends one charge and can trigger a half-strength, half-duration Rapid Killing bonus once every ten seconds.
- Shadow Arrow and Blood Arrow start Auto Shot against their target.
- Shadow Waste Bolt, Dark Arrow, and Bone Arrow are Verified and available through automatic tester collection grants.
