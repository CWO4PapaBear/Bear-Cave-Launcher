# Build Master and Trial Master Rewards

- Earn one Build Master's Trophy the first time your account completes each Featured Draft.
- Spend trophies in the Master reward shops or Vanity. Armor sets charge only for pieces still missing from your collection.
- Use Runes of Descension for Guardian of Time heirlooms in Vanity, including the seven deliverable armor caches. The Guardian's menu identifies pendants and Paladin librams.
