## Area 52 — Dungeons, Blademaster and Spartan Kick

- Added Normal, Heroic and Mythic support for 19 classic dungeons, including matching creature levels, health, loot tiers, encounter credit and group-queue support.
- Integrated dungeon boss damage/healing repairs, Dire Maul West fixes and Baroness Anastari possession fixes. Existing Area 52 loot rolls and progression are preserved.
- Repaired Blademaster's stack generation and its Devastate, Riposte and Divine Storm interactions. Connected Tactical Mastery's effects and repaired Divine Storm's higher-rank healing support. Blademaster, Tactical Mastery and Divine Storm now show **VERIFIED**.
- Spartan Kick now knocks back players and ordinary creatures in its supported stances. While equipped, the entire Kick ability has an independent **20-second cooldown**, separate from Shield Bash. Removing the enchant restores normal Kick without clearing the remaining cooldown. Boss and training-dummy knockback immunity remains.
- Preserved Stormwind training-dummy positions and linked formations while resolving dungeon spawn conflicts.
