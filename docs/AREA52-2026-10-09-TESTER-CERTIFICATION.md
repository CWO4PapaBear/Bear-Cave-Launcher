Testers can now certify VERIFIED abilities and talents directly in Character Advancement.

Right-click an eligible ability or talent, choose **Mark as CERTIFIED**, and confirm that you have checked:

1. It functions as expected.
2. It persists through logout and login.
3. It is removed from action bars when unlearned.

Press **CERTIFY** to save your result. Its status becomes green **CERTIFIED** for everyone immediately, and the server retains the certification for future sessions.

Existing learn/unlearn options and expanded SHIFT tooltip information remain available.

**Close the game and reopen your launcher, then press Play to receive the update.**
