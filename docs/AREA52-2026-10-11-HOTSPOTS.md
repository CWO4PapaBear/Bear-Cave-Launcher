## Area 52 — XP Hotspots and Client Improvements — October 11, 2026

- XP Hotspots now rotate through outdoor locations, granting **+150% monster-kill XP** while you are inside the active area. Each rotation lasts one hour, with three hotspots active at a time.
- Open the world map and select **Hotspots** to see active locations, remaining time, and coverage highlights. GMs can manage locations, rotation priority, bonus XP, duration, and announcements through the map interface.
- Added Mystic Enchant and talent-granted abilities to Advancement search, with their learning source shown in the SHIFT tooltip. Learned, VERIFIED granted abilities can now be submitted for certification.
- Restored right-click certification from the Mystic Enchant collection, including the tooltip hint.
- The certification leaderboard background is 75% transparent when not hovered. Click its Bear Cave icon to minimize or restore it; your choice is saved between sessions.
- Added scaling stats to the four previously statless heirloom rings, using the updated heirloom scaling curves.

**Close the game, reopen the Bear Cave Launcher, and press Play to install the matching client update before testing.**
