## Area 52 — Heirloom Scaling — October 11, 2026

- Rebalanced heirloom stat budgets for levels 68–80 so leveling equipment remains useful into Northrend. At level 80, category budgets target approximately 90% of basic dungeon gear; individual item stat mixes vary.
- Updated primary and secondary stats, weapon damage, and caster weapon spell-power curves. Levels 1–67, armor types and armor values, XP bonuses, and proc effects are unchanged.
- Included the 18 Felforged armor pieces that share these scaling curves.
- Example: Burnished Spaulders of Might now provide 38 Strength and 82 Stamina at level 80, up from 18 Strength and 39 Stamina.
