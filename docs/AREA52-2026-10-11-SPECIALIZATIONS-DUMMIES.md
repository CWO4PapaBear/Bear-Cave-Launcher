## Specializations and Training Dummies

**Live on Area 52 following the announced restart.**

- Specializations now save and restore your selected abilities, talents, primary stat, trained ranks, action bars, and Active Build settings per slot. Additional slots retain their existing unlock requirements.
- Switching uses the existing Specializations interface and supports its linked Mystic Enchant and equipment presets. Combat and other unsafe switching conditions are blocked.
- Training dummies now resist fear, confusion, charm, knockbacks (including Thunderstorm), and pulls so they stay at their testing positions. The Boss Simulator still accepts stun or disarm during its heavy-hit counter window.

**No client download is required for this server update. Log back in to test.**
