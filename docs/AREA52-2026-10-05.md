**Area 52 - Free Pick Alpha Dev: cumulative update**

Changes since the October 4 launcher 0.3.5 announcement:

**Launcher and client repair**
- Launcher 0.3.7 can repair the complete tested Area 52 client baseline: 503 required files, including the runtime, game archives, content data, cinematics, UI assets and Area 52 addons. This replaces the earlier partial-repair coverage in 0.3.5/0.3.6.
- Only missing or mismatched files are downloaded. Interrupted updates reuse verified download chunks. Original files are backed up; extra MPQs are quarantined with backups so they cannot override the tested client.
- The bottom bar fills left to right during download, installation and verification. It reaches 100% only after verification succeeds. Download totals and required disk space are shown.
- Personal settings, accounts, caches, screenshots and optional addons are not part of the distributed package. The launcher configures the Area 52 server connection.

**Build Architect and interface**
- Ordinary Active Builds now teach planned abilities and talents. Automatic learning works without a book; with automatic learning off, a training book is required. These three cases have been tested in game.
- Taller character-creation archetype descriptions, selection popups moved to the top of the screen, an expanded build-icon picker, and a Summary fallback for an otherwise blank Character Advancement pane are included.
- Player builds do not grant Mystic Enchants. Featured Draft Build progression and rewards remain separate work.

**Abilities and pets**
- Repaired spell-rank ladders used by training books, including Devastate and other affected abilities.
- Mana-forged Barrier now grants its linked Channeled Mana ability, including a backfill for existing learners.
- Integrated compatible upstream spell/effect repairs, including Charge, Sweeping Strikes, Beacon of Light, Eclipse, Revitalize and supporting summon mappings. Please continue testing individual spells.
- The base primary-pet family update is active for gameplay testing. Vanity-whistle verification and the separate second-pet system remain deferred.
- Weapon skills now automatically reach the level cap. Profession skills are not automatically maximized.

**How to update**
Close Ascension, reopen the launcher and confirm **0.3.7**, select **Area 52**, then **Check / Repair** and **Update**. Allow the final hash verification to finish. A complete replacement is about 43.3 GiB; your download depends on which files differ, and staging/backups require additional free space. If an older launcher will not update, contact us with `launcher-update.log`.

Bear Cave PTR retains its separate client channel. Victory Rush repairs and Skull Banner work are not included in this activation.
