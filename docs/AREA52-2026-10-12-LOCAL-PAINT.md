## XP Hotspot Painting Repair

Sorry for the frequent restarts, and thank you for your patience while we improve the testing experience.

- Brush and perimeter drawing now stay in a local preview until you click **Save Draft**.
- Undo stays local, and map outline rendering is lighter to reduce painting lag.
- Saving validates the complete drawing before applying it. If a save is rejected, your drawing remains available to correct and retry.

**Close the game, reopen the Bear Cave Launcher, and press Play to install the matching update before returning.**
