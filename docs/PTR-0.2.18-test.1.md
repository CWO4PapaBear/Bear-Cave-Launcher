# PTR 0.2.18-test.1 — Well Rested, Lootbot, creature textures and Hero levels

Changes since the previous Grappling Hook patch notes:

**Well Rested**
- Spend **15 continuous online minutes inside an inn**, alive and out of combat, to earn **8% additional monster-kill XP for two hours of online play**.
- **Resting** and **Well Rested** now have their original icons and visible countdowns. The 15-minute inn timer automatically restarts after completion; each completed cycle refreshes the reward to two hours without stacking it.
- Leaving the inn, entering combat, dying or logging out resets unfinished rest. The earned XP reward survives death and pauses while logged out. It does not increase quest or exploration XP.

**Lootbot**
- Fixed pickup from freshly killed creatures without first opening their loot manually.
- Companion pickups use the existing grey-item auto-sell system, without a second sale or duplicate item grant.
- Fully emptied corpses now lose their loot sparkle. Remaining loot stays available, and eligible corpses remain skinnable.
- Ownership, inventory capacity and group-loot restrictions remain enforced. Uncommon-or-better group loot remains for manual handling.

**Creature textures**
- Includes **64 creature display corrections** across ancient, ogre mage and wooly kodo families, plus **62 restored texture dependencies**.
- Gnarl in Ashenvale, Hamhock in the Stockades and Wooly Kodo in the Barrens were confirmed repaired in-game. Related variants are included; please report any remaining texture problems.

**Hero Advancement**
- Hero now uses the same ability-level requirements as Hybrid. Corrected **135 mismatched catalogue entries**; all **836 catalogue ability entries** pass the parity check.
- This includes level-one access for abilities normally unlocked through level 10 under the custom progression rules. Some previously incorrect Hero levels increase to match Hybrid. Classic progression and later-rank training remain unchanged.

**Update:** Close WoW → open launcher **0.3.1 or newer** → **Check / Repair → Update PTR → Play**. No new launcher ZIP is needed for players already on 0.3.1. Server changes are active; update the client to receive the icons, textures and ability-level correction.

Please test repeated inn cycles, reward timers after relogging, Lootbot with full bags or groups, and the corrected creatures. PTR only; Main is unchanged.
