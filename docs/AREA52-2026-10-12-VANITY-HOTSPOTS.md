# Area 52 — Vanity Incarnations & XP Hotspots

Close the game, reopen the Bear Cave Launcher, and press **Play** to install the matching client update before testing.

## Vanity incarnations

- Connected supported owned Vanity incarnations to their matching forms and summoned demons, including refreshing the appearance when the form or pet changes.
- Restored the spell-based visuals for Astral and Forest Guardian incarnations.

## XP Hotspot map tools

- Corrected continent and world-map filtering, including Bloodmyst Isle and expansion-map starting zones. Outland hotspots no longer appear over Azeroth's ocean.
- Added **New Draft** and **Save Draft** at the top of the editor. **Ctrl+click** on the map starts a circular draft at that location; GMs can switch it to a polygon.
- Matched the Locations and General Settings window sizes and repaired General Settings input borders.
- Moved the delete confirmation above the map and swapped the Teleport and Announce button positions.
