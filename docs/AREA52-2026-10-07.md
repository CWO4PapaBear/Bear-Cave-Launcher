**Everyone: fully close and reopen Bear Cave Launcher, then confirm version 0.3.8 in the footer.** Close Ascension before updating its client files.

**Simpler launch and updates**
- Select your realm and press **Play**. The launcher checks your client, downloads and repairs required files, then starts the game.
- A valid check is reused during the same session so Check/Repair, updating and Play do not repeatedly scan the entire unchanged client. Changed files and downloads are still verified.
- Check/Repair and Recover remain available under **Troubleshooting**.
- Once 0.3.8 is installed, future launcher updates show a visible updating window and progress bar, disable controls while updating, then reopen the updated launcher.
- **This first upgrade may still use the older launcher's silent updater.** Allow it time to finish and reopen; avoid starting multiple copies.
- The published Area 52 manifest URL has been corrected. The recent “Unexpected manifest URL” error was a publishing issue, not evidence that a fresh COACore client is incompatible.

**Recent Area 52 changes available for testing**
- Ability and talent resets cost **250 Runes of Ascension**. Reset All keeps your Active Build selected and turns off Auto-Learn Spells.
- Chromie's Prestige now supports Area 52 Free Pick Hero characters, subject to its normal eligibility requirements.
- Unlearning an ability now clears its obsolete action-bar buttons, including Reset All.

Please report update failures with your launcher version, the exact message, and launcher-update.log. Bear Cave PTR and Area 52 continue to use separate client folders and update channels.
