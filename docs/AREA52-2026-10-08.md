# Area 52 — Mystic Scrolls and Archetype Vanity — October 8, 2026

Close Ascension, fully close and reopen the Bear Cave Launcher, select Area 52, and press Play to check and install the update before launching. This update changes client content; it does not require a new launcher version.

## Mystic Scrolls
- Unidentified Mystic Scrolls now produce a physical, tradable scroll for a randomly selected Mystic Enchant, with its matching icon. Identifying a scroll does not learn the enchant.
- Right-click an identified scroll near a Mystic Altar to open the Add to Collection confirmation. Learning consumes the scroll and one Mystic Extract, with the existing Rune of Descension purchase fallback.
- Random rerolls are connected in the Enchant Scrolls interface, using the existing price and Rune of Descension currency, with native reveal effects and sounds.
- All 3,822 catalog entries have scroll item mappings. This does not certify every enchant's combat behavior.

## Archetype Vanity
- Archetype bundles grant equippable vanity pieces and automatically unlock their Wardrobe appearances.
- Bundle ownership is persistent and searchable in Vanity, with retrieval available later.
- Collection messages identify the archetype. Bundles use their archetype icons.
- Naturalist now includes its helmet in the set and character-creation preview.

The Area 52 server package is active. Please test scroll identification, collection confirmations, altar rerolls, and vanity bundle retrieval, and report any issues. Bear Cave PTR remains shut down.
