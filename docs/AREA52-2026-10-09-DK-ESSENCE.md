# Area 52 — Death Knight Ability Essence Costs

Level-71+ Death Knight abilities now have reduced Ability Essence costs:

- 46 entries reduced from 4 to 2.
- Dancing Rune Weapon, Summon Gargoyle, and Anti-Magic Zone reduced from 6 to 3.
- Presence Mastery, Blood Presence, Frost Presence, and Unholy Presence reduced from 2 to 1.

Rarity-gem costs and level requirements are unchanged.

Close the game and reopen your launcher, then press Play to receive the matching client update.
