## Area 52 - Free Resets and Active Builds

- Removing abilities or talents is now free, including both Reset All buttons. Points are refunded through the Advancement menu.
- Ability and talent changes are blocked during combat and inside battlegrounds or arenas.
- Unlearning cleans up associated self-cast buffs, including linked and triggered effects.
- Active Builds automatically apply their selected Primary Stat. With Auto-Learn Spells enabled, eligible planned abilities and talents are learned through your current level.
- Reset All keeps your Active Build selected and turns off Auto-Learn Spells.
- The Male Hoplite vanity bundle and Wardrobe set now include Hidden Chest. Existing bundle owners can reclaim and reopen the bundle.

Close the game and reopen the Bear Cave Launcher, then press Play to receive the matching client update.
